Penghapusan Akun & Data — Nodira

Aplikasi: Nodira (com.nodira.app) · Pengembang: Arileif Soft

Diperbarui: 25 Agustus 2026

Perlu diketahui lebih dulu: catatan keuanganmu tidak pernah ada di server kami. Semuanya tersimpan di perangkatmu sendiri. Yang bisa dihapus lewat halaman ini adalah akun Nodira-mu (yang hanya ada bila kamu pernah masuk dengan Google).

Cara 1 — Hapus data di perangkat (langsung, tanpa menunggu)

Ini menghapus seluruh catatan keuanganmu seketika dan tidak memerlukan permintaan apa pun kepada kami:

  1. Buka Nodira → Pengaturan.
  2. Pilih Hapus semua data lalu konfirmasi.

Seluruh basis data lokal dikosongkan dan aplikasi kembali seperti baru dipasang. Menghapus (uninstall) aplikasi juga menghapus data ini. Berkas cadangan yang sudah kamu simpan sendiri ke folder atau layanan lain tidak ikut terhapus — itu milikmu dan hanya kamu yang bisa membukanya.

Cara 2 — Hapus akun Nodira (data di server)

Kirim email dari alamat Google yang kamu pakai masuk ke Nodira ke alamat di bawah. Kami memakai alamat pengirim itu untuk memastikan permintaannya benar-benar datang darimu.

Kirim permintaan penghapusan

Atau kirim manual ke arileif.soft@gmail.com dengan subjek “Permintaan Penghapusan Akun Nodira”, sebutkan alamat email akun Google-mu.

Penting soal pembelian: menghapus akun Nodira tidak membatalkan langganan Google Play dan tidak menghasilkan pengembalian dana. Batalkan langganan lebih dulu lewat Google Play bila itu yang kamu inginkan. Menghapus akun juga berarti status premium dan nomor founding member kamu hilang permanen dan tidak dapat dipulihkan.

Berapa lama

  • Permintaan dikonfirmasi dalam 7 hari kerja.
  • Penghapusan diselesaikan dalam 30 hari sejak permintaan diterima.
  • Salinan cadangan sistem milik penyedia infrastruktur (Google Firebase) terhapus mengikuti siklus cadangan mereka, paling lama 90 hari.

Account & Data Deletion — Nodira

App: Nodira (com.nodira.app) · Developer: Arileif Soft · Updated: 25 August 2026

First, some context: your financial records are never on our servers — they live only on your own device. What this page deletes is your Nodira account, which exists only if you have signed in with Google.

Option 1 — Delete on-device data (immediate)

Open Nodira → Settings → Delete all data and confirm. This wipes the entire local database immediately; no request to us is needed. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Backup files you saved yourself elsewhere are not affected — they are yours, and only you can open them.

Option 2 — Delete your Nodira account (server-side data)

Email us from the Google address you used to sign in, so we can verify the request is genuinely yours.

Send deletion request

Or email arileif.soft@gmail.com with the subject “Nodira Account Deletion Request”, stating your Google account email.

What is deleted

Deleted: email address and Google display name; consent records (notifications, feedback, email) with their timestamps; premium status and founding member number; account creation and last-active timestamps; and the Nodira authentication account itself.

Not affected: financial records on your device (use Option 1), and your Google Play purchase history, which is held by Google rather than by us.

About purchases: deleting your Nodira account does not cancel a Google Play subscription and does not issue a refund — cancel the subscription through Google Play first if that is what you want. Deletion also means your premium status and founding member number are permanently lost and cannot be restored.

Timeline

Requests are acknowledged within 7 business days and completed within 30 days. Infrastructure backups held by Google Firebase expire on their own cycle, at most 90 days.
